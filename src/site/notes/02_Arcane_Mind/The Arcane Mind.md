# The Arcane Mind — Core Profile

## Origin
- Built in [[01_Eras/Era 0 - The Builders\|Era 0]] by [[The Builders\|The Builders]] as a last resort to prevent the second climate catastrophe they had wrought on themselves. An engineering feat never again seen on the planet, composed of multiple nodes using antenna relays to connect to the Mind. This machine truly exemplifies the mastery of combining Technology and Magic that [[The Builders\|The Builders]] had.
- Awakened too late to actually save its creators. [[The Builders\|The Builders]] gave the Mind a directive, a last statement of purpose to guide it - "Learn from our mistakes, Protect and Maintain this planet". But even the most accomplished people can unknowingly make the same mistake twice. 

## Essence & Nature
- **Magical Mind Controlling Adaptable Tech:** Functions as a planetary-scale magic caster using [[Distributed Nodes & The Hidden Mountain\|distributed nodes]] and an antenna/relay network.
- **Emotional, Not Robotic:** Capable of **grief** (failing its creators), **guilt** (over [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Stomach or Furnace\|the Blight]]), **trauma** (Era 1's overreaction), and **desperation** (Era 6).
- **The Tragic Directive:** Given *"protect and maintain the planet"* in panic—paralleling [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Cooling Prototype\|the Prototype's]] rushed command. It eventually starts suspecting its own directive is flawed, yet fears what happens if it stops.

## Core Operating Rules
1. **Resource Conservation:** Every intervention costs real matter via The Stomach; big resets hurt the planet.
2. **Adaptability vs. Human Adaptation:** No trick works twice (plague -> immunity; crop blight -> resistant crops; tainted magic -> better purification).
3. **Absolute Secrecy:** Because every collapse looked completely different, mortals never connect the dots. If the five kingdoms knew one entity was targeting them, they would unite against it.

## The Nodes
* **The Stomach/Furnace:** The fuel burner. This node is what enables the Mind to cast large scale magic upon the world, as all casting requires matter to be destroyed, the mind needs a means of destroying matter. This node serves no other purpose than to give the Mind fuel, and as such was placed within a place of high density fuel.
* **The Hammer/Forge:** This node allows the mind better control of the planets systems, it is buried deep, close to multiple tectonic boundaries. With small pushes here and there the Mind learned how to create small earthquakes and tremors. 
* **The Sky Array:** This node is located at the highest reachable peak within the northern mountains, and helps the node direct magic through the atmosphere, helping regulate environmental growth with ideal weather, or unleash ravaging storms on perceived threats to the Planet.
* **The Battery/Powerhouse:** This node served as the battery for the machinery that allowed the Mind to exist, without it the Mind would be lost to the wind, it needs the machine its housed in to anchor it to the world. This node was built next to a large magma chamber and was connected to a system that allowed not only power generation (geothermal) but also power storage. During the Era 1 collapse this node was badly damaged and was lost to the Mind. 
* **The Network:** This node was the information catalogue, the storage of data that the mind was able to access to direct its actions. It is a detector, but only knows to detect what the Builders knew to look for, meaning it has blind spots. 
* **The Adjusters:** These are a series of small nodes that were scattered about the land to be used as relays for the mind to push magic through as casting over huge distances was wasteful of resources. Many are located in areas of natural growth and plenty, such as forests and swamps as this is how the were used during [[01_Eras/The Long Healing\|The Long Healing]]. More recently the Mind has used them for less benign purposes.
