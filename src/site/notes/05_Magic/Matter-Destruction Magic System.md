## Matter-Destruction Magic System

## Core Principles
- **The Basis/Physics:** When matter (magical component) is destroyed, it creates huge amounts of energy (conservation) which if contained in a small enough space can fuse to increase the output and be redirected in a different manner 
- **The Fantasy:** That the peoples of this world are able to contain and control this destruction of matter and fusion to redirect it as magic.
- **The Flavour:** The magical component used/destroyed will define the outcome of a spell. For example, charcoal may be used to create a smoke of fire spell. 
- **The Creativity:** How the component is used exactly is up to the whim of the caster, whether for offense, defense, utility or everyday tasks. 

## Components
- Components are the required materials that a caster needs to be able to cast, and these can range massively depending on the intended outcome of the spell.
- Components are defined into three categories: Raw Ingredients, Regular Component, Refined Component. These offer a level of benefit or detriment to the caster trying to use them. A caster using a Refined Component such as Purified Water will have an easy time containing the energy and controlling the fusion, so can cast a more powerful water jet with those few drops. A caster using a regular Component such as Drinking Water (not purified, but still clean) will cast a water jet with slightly less power and control compared to with Purified Water. And casting with a Raw Ingredient such as Muddy Water nearby will have a much harder time making that same clean water jet and it will never be as powerful or easy to control. The basis for this is that the cleaner the component, the more 'known' it is and the closer it is to the outcome spell being cast (Pure Water into Pure Water is easier). A Raw Ingredient will have lots of unknowns and extra elements in it which upon destruction will dilute the known and destabalise the process, making it much harder. 
- Components have an 'affinity' to certain types of magic, a rock will be easier to reform as a rock based spell but almost impossible to turn into a burst of fire. This affinity helps drive the creativeness of casters in a tight situation, it also means that using a well known spell like a jet of water, may not be the best option if all you have is a muddy puddle nearby, and instead trying to create a sludge bomb will be easier due to the affinity of the component, sometimes adjusting the intended outcome will make the casting less dangerous. Trying to convert the muddy water into a clean jet will drastically reduce the output as much of the energy will not redirect properly and the water jet will splutter rather than shoot. 


## Skill Scaling (Dual Progression)
1. **Conversion Efficiency (Skill):** How much energy a caster extracts per gram of component. This is the amount of energy converted (higher energy efficiency) as well as the ability to Fuse the energy at a greater level to produce a greater outcome. Low Skill means a few drops of water turns into a small jet of water, high Skill means a few drops of water becomes a geyser.
2. **Component Capacity (Strength):** How much pure matter a caster can safely convert at once without losing containment. Strength is in essence brute forcing the components from destruction into containment. The more component destroyed, the more energy available without using skill to fuse at a higher rate, and therefore a greater outcome. A low Strength means only a few drops of water can be destroyed to create a water jet, a high Strength means a bathtub of water can be destroyed to be thrown out as a more powerful water jet.
3. **Crossover:** These two skills work like building muscle mass and muscle strength, they do build together, but if you only focus on one the other will not progress nearly as far. This creates a lot of variance in how casters function in every environment.

## Destabalisation
- In essence, this process is caused by the caster trying to hold or fuse too much energy. Energy vibrates rapidly, especially when trapped into such a tight space, and so some of that vibration is pushed back into the caster who has overextended. As such a caster who is Destabalising is often seen to vibrate and shift as their body absorbs some of the energy. A stronger and or more skilled caster has more control or power to prevent this from happening unless they try something way more extreme compared to what they've done before.
- Casters who try to take in too much matter for destruction or try to cast at a fusion rate beyond their ability risk destabalising and causing potentially significant harm to themselves as a portion of the energy may be dumped inward, and some out. This means that novices are a greater threat to themselves than to everyone around them, but being near a caster when they destabalise in not safe either. 

## Creativity Is Key
- This magic system does not use specific spells as such, though many obvious ones are available to any caster with the skill and components handy. Rather this system is about creativity within the confines. A caster with a small piece of pure granite can destroy the component and with enough skill, create a solid granite shield for their party. But using that same piece of granite could create a granite coating over an enemies weapon or armour, or instead recreate the same size piece of granite and put all the excess energy into velocity, creating a rock bullet. The confines are the components, the creativity of the caster, and the skill/strength required to manage it. Anything can technically be a component depending on what outcome the caster is after, but a piece of rock will not be turned into a fireball unless the caster is extremely skilled at conversion, which is a very rare and generally unknown skill. Casters need to figure out what components are good for what, especially if they find themselves stuck in a corner with no components on them, versatility and creativity are rewarded through this system.
- This magic has helped to define and drive the technology of the era, mage rifles helped bring about the invention of regular rifles as an inventive caster found that a long thin barrel allowed him to cast his spells at a greater range than he could manage himself, so many weaker mages have found ingenious means of using technology to enhance their casting.
