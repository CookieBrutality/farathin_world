# The Long Healing (~10,000 Years)

Following the death of [[06_Peoples/The Builders (Era 0)\|the Builders]], the grieving [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Arcane Mind - Core Profile\|Arcane Mind]] spent roughly **10,000 years** alone:
- Shut down and dismantled [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Cooling Prototype\|The Cooling Prototype]].
- Gently thawed the Ice Age and stabilized the planet's erratic climate.
- Experimented with how to exert its planetary-caster abilities through its [[02_Arcane_Mind/Distributed Nodes & The Hidden Mountain\|distributed nodes]].
- Arcane Mind started experimenting with environmental magic, faster forest regrowth etc. Side effects at the time unknown.

*Note: Earth benchmark comparison—going from near-extinction/stone-age conditions back to organized metallurgy and cities takes ~10,000–12,000 years.*
