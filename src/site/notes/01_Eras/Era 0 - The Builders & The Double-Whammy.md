# Era 0: The Builders

## Summary
[[06_Peoples/The Builders (Era 0)\|The Builders]]—a hyper-advanced civilization that deeply understood [[05_Magic/Matter-Destruction Magic System\|matter-to-energy conversion]]—pushed the planet into a **runaway climate catastrophe** before accidentally finishing themselves off with a rushed geo-engineering fix.

## Sequence of Collapse
1. **First Blow — Runaway Greenhouse:** Casting advanced technological leaps started global warming with mass fuel burning to create great cities and machines. By the time they knew something was wrong it was too late, and a runaway greenhouse effect triggered mega-droughts, dead zones, and hyper-storms, shattering cities and wiping out huge portions of the population.
2. **The Flawed Prototype:** Desperate to halt melting ice caps, some of the surviving Builders built and deployed [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Cooling Prototype\|The Cooling Prototype]] as a means of reversing the damage they had done. But the desperation of the survivors and their unusual lack of knowledge meant that a safety limit was not set. The machine was left in the Artic north to do its task. 
3. **Second Blow — Runaway Ice Age:** Distracted by surviving storms and famine, the Builders realized too late that the Prototype triggered an **albedo flip**, plunging the battered world into a lethal Ice Age.
4. **Ignition of the Arcane Mind:** As the prototype was starting to flip the climate, some of the Builders pooled their resources and knowledge into a last minute bid to counter it - the [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Arcane Mind - Core Profile\|Arcane Mind]]. The [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Arcane Mind - Core Profile\|Arcane Mind]] was a culmination of all the knowledge of magic and technology that [[The Builders\|The Builders]] had. The final piece being the power of an entire lightning strike harnessed by the most accomplished Caster of the era, to kickstart the machine and awaken the sentient magical mind within. The mind was given a directive by the last remaining builders before they perished to the climate catastrophe they had brought upon themselves - (*"protect and maintain the planet"*). The [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Arcane Mind - Core Profile\|Arcane Mind]] was too late to prevent the collapse of the builders, and went into [[01_Eras/The Long Healing\|The Long Healing]] as a sole survivor of their knowledge and expertise.
