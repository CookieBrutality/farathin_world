# Era 1: The Great Scar & The Soaked Dunes

## Summary
A new and growing civilisation. Agriculture had taken off, and the slopes of a large, mostly inactive volcano provided the perfect fertile land to grow food on. Large chunk of the entire civilisation is based around the volcano. They had just started forging on a higher scale, producing mostly tools, and using some magic to enhance the process, but limited in planetary impact. The Mind was not actively aware of them, 10,000 years it had healed the planet and then watched and maintained, but it didn't really know what it was protecting the planet from at that point. And then it suddenly noticed the civilisation, not only was it advancing technologically, it was also sat right next to one of the nodes. The Mind reacted, uncertain but panicked, and decided the magma chamber below the volcano could be used to set the civilisation back, stop them from advancing to a full blown planetary threat. But in its panic, the mind miscalculated, it superheated and fractured the crust around a massive magma chamber, it casted way beyond what it meant to, and destroyed a huge chunk of matter around [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Stomach or Furnace\|The Stomach or Furnace]], and causing an eruption that then led to a **cataclysmic caldera collapse** as the massive magma chamber emptied out. The edge of the collapse near the ocean led to the creation of [[07_Locations/The Soaked Dunes\|The Soaked Dunes]]. Ash and sulfur triggered a **5–10 year volcanic winter and famine**, wiping out almost all survivors and forcing a ~6,000-year recovery.

## Lasting Impact
- **The Wasteland:** Era 1 ending burned through enormous amounts of matter, and in doing so it practically drained the huge bog that [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Stomach or Furnace\|The Stomach or Furnace]] was located under practically overnight. In doing so the bog could not recover, and so [[The Crater\|The Crater]] formed and began to grow.
- **Ruins:** Drowned stone megastructures and the central volcanic ring-island in the Soaked Dunes. The eruption wiped out much of the Era 1 civilisation and the layers of ash preserved some of the lives though much was broken apart in the collapse. The subsequent flooding helped preserve some of the remaining intact ashen ruins, forming a crust over the ash and eventually helping coral reefs to grow and thrive all over the dunes. 
- **The Damaged Node:** The sheer size of the eruption badly damaged [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Powerhouse and Battery\|The Powerhouse]] which was suited not far from the main volcanic conduit and prevented [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Arcane Mind\|The Arcane Mind]] from being able to produce more power. [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Powerhouse and Battery\|The Battery]] survived as it was located in a secure location further from the Volcano, however it was submerged during the subsequent collapse and flooding. Its location is unknown, but it is the only remaining source of power now for [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Arcane Mind\|The Arcane Mind]].
- **Healing and Evolution:** Post collapse, [[02_Arcane_Mind/The Arcane Mind\|The Arcane Mind]] pours magic into the dunes to help regrow the planet, much like it had during the long healing. This helps life to thrive and (unknowingly to the Mind) creates the evolution of the Galapa.
