Across **Farathin**, five kingdoms are locked in a relentless struggle for dominance—fighting border skirmishes, racing to out-invent one another, and seizing every edge they can find, while scattered independent territories struggle not to drown. Power in this age is fueled by a volatile magic of conservation: destroying matter releases massive bursts of elemental energy, which skilled Casters can redirect into awe inspiring powers. A technological boom born from Caster inventiveness has led to rifles and small firearms becoming more commonplace as Casters continue to invent new technology to aid themselves, and unintentionally push society further forwards. Yet as the kingdoms surge onward, strange natural disasters, an ancient wasteland beginning to creep outward, and unfamiliar creatures are closing in on the hearts of civilization.

Beneath the smoke of industry and these rising dangers lies a deeper mystery. Scattered across the land are the ruins of unknown ancient civilizations—and no two sets look alike. As the five kingdoms push deeper into forgotten vaults to scavenge lost edges for their power struggle, heroes, explorers, and survivors must uncover what is driving the world's sudden fury before civilization as they know it becomes just another forgotten era.
